MySpace is a secure digital document vault with AES-256-GCM encryption, facial biometric authentication with anti-spoofing liveness detection, MFA verification, expiry monitoring and time-limited QR code sharing.
MySpace is a personal digital management platform designed to help users keep their important information, files, and documents organized in one convenient place. Instead of depending on multiple applications or scattered folders, users can use MySpace as a centralized space for managing their digital content. The application is designed with a simple interface so that users can easily add, access, organize, and manage their information without unnecessary complexity.

The application supports different methods for adding content, giving users flexibility based on their needs. Users can:

Upload files directly from their device.
Capture and save photos or videos.
Record new voice clips or select existing audio files.
Paste supported content from the device clipboard.

MySpace also provides a multi-person profile system for users who need to manage information belonging to different people. Each person can have an independent profile where their documents and other information are stored separately. Users can access an individual profile and manage its content without mixing it with information belonging to another person. This makes the application useful for managing personal, family, educational, or professional information in an organized manner.

The platform includes convenient tools for accessing and sharing stored information. Users can choose how they want to share their files depending on the situation, including:

Direct sharing for quick file transfer.
QR-based sharing for convenient access through a QR code.
Export for downloading stored files to the device.
Controlled sharing options based on the user's requirements.

MySpace focuses on creating a simple, accessible, and user-friendly digital experience while giving users greater control over their information. Its language support can make the platform easier to use for people with different language preferences. The combination of organized profiles, flexible content input, document management, sharing, and export capabilities makes MySpace suitable for students, families, professionals, and everyday users. The main objective of MySpace is to provide a convenient personal digital space where important information can be managed efficiently, accessed when needed, and shared through simple and practical methods.
